Kleinmond App Studio

Local apps for local people • From the slipway to the coffee table • Keeping Kleinmond connected

Kleinmond App Studio builds simple, useful tools for our community – for the boaters and fishermen, the coffee-morning crowd, the clubs and the families. Big buttons, no sign-ups, and the community apps below are free for everyone.
